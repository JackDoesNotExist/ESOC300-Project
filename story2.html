First Month of Treatment


My treatment plan was to recieve treatment over the course of three months. Each month I had three weeks on chemotherapy and one week off. The very first time I recieved treatment was after the surgery where I recieved my port. I was still in the hospital because it was the very same day and I had to spend at least the night there. To be fully honest, I really do not remember much of that night. One of the key things I remember was the nausea. The medication made me very nauseous. I spent a good portion of the night throwing up and trying to sleep. I think some more personal things happened as well, but I had to learn those things from my mom that told my afterwards. This whole ordeal took place during COVID so only one parent was allowed to be with me, both during my first treatment and all the postceding appointments.


I believe it was around the end of the first month that I started to lose my hair. It was pretty uncomfortable and my scalp was getting sensitive. I decided to shave my head because I was tired of finding hair everywhere. Honestly, I was also kind of curious about short hair. My dad not only shaved my head for me, but he also shaved his head. I've never been great at expressing my emotions, but I think he knew that I appreciated it. I wasn't really embarrased about being bald, but my mom did get me these soft skull caps. Think beanie but not as insulated. They were really nice and kept my head warm, but not too warm.
