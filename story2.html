First Month of Treatment


My treatment plan was to recieve treatment over the course of three months. Each month I had three weeks on chemotherapy and one week off. The very first time I recieved treatment was after the surgery where I recieved my port. I was still in the hospital because it was the very same day and I had to spend at least the night there. To be fully honest, I really do not remember much of that night. One of the key things I remember was the nausea. The medication made me very nauseous. I spent a good portion of the night throwing up and trying to sleep. I think some more personal things happened as well, but I had to learn those things from my mom that told my afterwards.
