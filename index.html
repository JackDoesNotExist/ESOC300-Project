Intro & Guide


This is my project! It combines text and a game I created using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The game is a clicker game where you build a town in the Wild West! In favor of time, I had to forfeit some of the elements I wanted to add that would make the western theme clearer, but I think it still works. I'll try not to spoil it too much here, but the game represents how my love of video games stayed constant throughout my experience with cancer. The game is purposefully unrelated to the theme of the story. The jarring shift represents that, however tense things got, though I may not have felt very anxious at all, I was still just playing video games. It's meant to be distracting on purpose.


Now, for the short guide! Each section of the game has a different quest you must complete (you can skip it by just hitting the "Next" button, just in case time is of the essence) to continue reading the story. Before working on the first quest, I recommend getting "Better Hammers" to at least level 3. Each level of "Better Hammers" increases money from clicking by 1 per click. The "Taxes" upgrade generates $0.1 per level. Each building increases the relevant worker cap by 5.
