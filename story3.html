Second Month of Treatment


I was diagnosed in December, during winter break. That means I was in school while recieving treatment. However, if there was one good thing that came from COVID, it was online schooling. The reason that was good was because I didn't have to physically be at school. I was already tired from Chemotherapy so I was glad to be able to just have my camera off when my teachers allowed it. Speaking of teachers, Banner had a teacher meet with me and ask if I needed any help with my classes or if I needed her to speak with any of my teachers at school. They also gave me an IPad to KEEP to use so I could work on my classes while recieving treatment.
