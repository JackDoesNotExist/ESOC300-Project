Third Month of Treatment


A good chunk of my treatment was spent trying to find a medicine that would cut down the nausea I was feeling. The nausea really sucked, which I think is why it's one of the first things I remember when looking back at my treatment. It didn't feel like regular nausea, like what you feel on a car ride or something comparable. There was truly nothing I could do to lessen the nausea. None of the usual remedies, like ginger ale, water, or watching your posture, helped. Thankfully, we did eventually find a medication that helped with that nausea, though it was towards the end of my treatment.
